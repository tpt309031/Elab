# Research integrity and volume audit release

## Fixes

- Repair weekly publication failures caused by double-counted or overwritten
  immutable forecast quota reservations. Preserve SIDEWAY <=8 and NO CALL <=4.
- Make monthly allocation chronological: later predictions cannot change earlier
  decisions. Remove overlapping policy observations and mismatched calibrators.
- Restore live technical features with timestamp-based availability alignment.
- Grade immutable daily forecasts after 03:00 UTC, retain the scheduled 03:20 UTC
  refresh, and detect overdue grades and stale walk-forward research in health.
- Keep next-day execution gates separate from long-range calendar outlooks.

## New research and interface

- Real Binance spot BTC/USDT volume, USDT turnover, trades and taker flow,
  including 3,313 complete UTC days through September 11, 2026.
- Independent 1D / 3D / 5D large-move probabilities and forward-only evaluation:
  precision, recall, false alarms, missed events and Brier skill against base rate.
- Compare Index + Astro, Hybrid and Hybrid + Volume without pooling live
  forecasts and retrospective walk-forward tests.
- Responsive Volume & Event Lab, chart volume pane, dark tooltips, filtered
  forecast metrics and explicit probability/grade labels.
- Bounded training workers, recoverable lane checkpoints and verification before
  atomic artifact replacement.

## Results and limitations

Full refit: January 2024 to September 10, 2026, 984 daily observations per lane.
Calendar / Full Hybrid ensemble directional accuracy is 48.12% / 48.94%, with
negative after-cost expectancy. The 70% objective is not achieved. Selected
large-move models do not demonstrate useful alert precision at the fixed threshold.
This release improves correctness and measurement, not proven trading profitability.
The daily refresh includes the September 11 candle and grades. All 130 previously
published directional forecasts are preserved; 126 are evaluated and four pending.
Execution remains gated. Private Index/Astro history assumes prepublication because
timestamped source versions are unavailable; it is not a verified live record.

49 regression tests passed; six optional deep-learning tests skipped locally.
